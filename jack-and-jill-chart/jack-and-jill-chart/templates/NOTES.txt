This chart deploys multiple components. To access a component without ingress:

  export POD=$(kubectl get pod -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }} -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -o jsonpath='{.items[0].metadata.name}')
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward $POD 8080:8080

If ingress is enabled per component, open the configured host(s).
